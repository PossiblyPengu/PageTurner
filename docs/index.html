Settings

Appearance

Theme

Pure black backgrounds switch OLED pixels off, which saves battery on iPhone. Applies when the app is dark; the reader's Night theme becomes true black too.

Read Aloud

Quality
Speed
1×

Neural voices sound more natural and keep reading with the screen locked. Choosing one downloads it once (about 60 MB) and keeps it on this device, so it works offline and stays through app updates. Auto picks neural on iPhone and iPad.

Metadata

Metadata search sends a book's title and author to Google Books and Open Library; covers download from those sites. Turn this off for a fully offline app — automatic lookups stop and manual search is disabled. Everything else stays on-device.

BookMaster

Link a BookMaster account to send your reading progress to bookmaster.pages.dev as you read, so your tracker stays current. Notifications wake this device when something waits for you — the notice itself is fetched after the wake, so its text never passes through the push service. Nothing is sent until you link — everything else stays on-device.

Library

A backup holds every book, cover and reading position in one file you can restore here or on another device.
