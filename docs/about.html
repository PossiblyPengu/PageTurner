Pageturner

Reads and plays the books you already have.

  • EPUB, PDF, comics (CBZ/CBR) and audiobooks, with page turns that follow your finger.
  • Read aloud with on-device voices, highlights and notes, and reading statistics kept on your phone.
  • Optional: link BookMaster to keep your reading in sync and chat with another reader; connect Google Drive to add books from it and back your library up.
  • No account, no ads, no tracking. Your library lives on your device.
